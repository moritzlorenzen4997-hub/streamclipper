Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Summary

StreamClipper runs entirely on the operator's own computer. It has no servers, no user accounts and no analytics. It does not sell or share personal data.

2. Data the app handles

  • Platform access tokens (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram) for the operator's own accounts. They are stored only in a local file on the operator's computer and are used solely to upload approved clips.
  • TikTok basic profile info (user.info.basic), such as the account's open ID, used only to confirm which account is authorized. It is not stored beyond the token file.
  • Public live-stream video and chat messages, processed locally to detect highlights. Raw recordings are deleted automatically after a short rolling window (about 30 minutes). Chat messages are counted for activity and not linked to or stored with user identities.

3. Data sharing

Clips are sent only to the platforms the operator chooses (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram), using their official APIs. No data is sent to any other third party.

4. Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the settings of each platform. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all tokens and clips.
